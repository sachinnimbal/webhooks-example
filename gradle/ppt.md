## 🎯 KEY INTERACTION PRINCIPLES:

### 1. **Use Their Names**
- Makes it personal
- Keeps them alert
- Builds rapport

### 2. **Physical Movement**
- Don't stand in one spot
- Move toward audience
- Use whole stage/room

### 3. **Ask Questions Constantly**
- "Does this make sense?"
- "Who's done this before?"
- "Can you guess what happens next?"

### 4. **Wait for Answers**
- Don't answer your own questions!
- Let silence build
- Someone will speak up

### 5. **React Authentically**
- Laugh at their jokes
- Acknowledge good questions
- Show genuine excitement

### 6. **Use Props**
- Your phone
- Whiteboard
- Even a pen as "buzzer"

### 7. **Vary Your Voice**
- Loud for excitement
- Quiet for important points
- Different voices for characters

### 8. **Tell Stories**
- Personal experiences
- Shared team moments
- Funny incidents

---

## 💪 CONFIDENCE TRICKS:

### Before Each Slide:
1. **Take a breath**
2. **Make eye contact**
3. **Smile**
4. **Start with energy**

### If You Forget:
- **Look at slide** - it'll remind you
- **Say:** "Actually, let me show you..." (buy time)
- **Keep going** - they don't know you forgot!

### If They Look Confused:
- **STOP immediately**
- **Ask:** "Does this make sense so far?"
- **Rephrase** using different words
- **Give another example**

### If They Look Bored:
- **Speed up**
- **Skip theoretical parts**
- **Jump to demo** early

### ✅ Advantages of Webhooks

**Real-time updates** – Webhooks send data instantly when something happens, so there’s no delay.

**No repeated API calls** – Your app doesn’t have to keep asking the server for updates, which saves time and processing power.

**Saves bandwidth & server load** – Since fewer requests are made, both your app and the server handle less traffic.

**Easy automation & integration** – Webhooks can trigger actions automatically, like sending emails or updating databases.

**Lightweight & scalable** – They use simple HTTP requests, making them efficient and easy to scale for large systems.

### ❌ Disadvantages of Webhooks

**No guaranteed delivery** – If your server is offline when a webhook is sent, you might miss the notification.

**Needs secure setup** – You must use HTTPS and verify signatures to prevent fake or malicious data.

**Harder to debug** – Since the event comes from an external source, finding issues or failures can be tricky.

**Requires public URL access** – The webhook endpoint must be available on the internet, not just on your local system.

**Events may arrive late or out of order** – Sometimes multiple notifications might not reach you in the correct sequence.
